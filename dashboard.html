SMS marketing channel — almost completely untapped

99.5% of players (16,652 of 16,739) have a phone number on file.
Mailchimp supports SMS campaigns. Phone numbers are sitting there. SMS gets ~98% open rates vs ~25% for email. For win-back of lapsed regulars and high-LTV 35-54 segments, SMS is likely a 5-10x more effective channel than email — particularly for time-sensitive offers ("£10 off this weekend"). Caveat: SMS marketing requires explicit consent under UK PECR/GDPR. The Playtomic accepts_commercial_communications flag may or may not cover SMS specifically — worth confirming with Seb before sending.

Strategic takeaways

1. Capacity-constrained sites: Wimbledon (84%), Corsham (84%), Shepton (75%). Marketing spend here is wasted. Levers are pricing yield, lessons revenue, and (where physically possible) extending hours. 2. Headroom sites: Sedlescombe (58%), Basingstoke (55%), Boston (42% with maintenance). Marketing spend belongs here. 3. 52% one-and-done retention problem. The lapsed customer email automation is the single biggest near-term lever. 4. 9.6% marketing opt-in is the bottleneck behind the lapsed-customer fix. Get this above 20% and the lapsed list more than doubles. 5. Chain isn't yet a chain. Only 201 of 16,739 unique players (1.2%) are registered at 2+ venues. National membership + cross-promotion would be a free uplift. 6. Female participation is 17%. Sedlescombe is the outlier at 25% — worth understanding what's working there. Industry benchmark for indoor padel is closer to 30-35%, so there's room. 7. 35-54 is your highest LTV segment (£148-154/player over 90 days). Retention spend should over-index on this group. 8. SMS is your sleeping giant. 99.5% phone coverage, almost certainly underused. Consent rules apply but worth investigating with Mailchimp + Seb.
